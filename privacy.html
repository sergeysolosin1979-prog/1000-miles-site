1000 Miles · Your privacy

Privacy Policy

One step at a time. A clear explanation of what happens to your information.

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Scope and who provides the app

This policy covers the current 1000 Miles Android app (package com.miles1000.app), provided by the developer operating the GitHub account sergeysolosin1979-prog, and this public website. The app is preparing for Google Play closed testing.

Information we collect

The app does not collect or transmit your personal information to the developer. It has no account or login, and it does not request Android internet permission or send notes to a backend.

Information you enter is processed on your device. This includes note titles, comments, categories, creation and due or expected dates, completion status and dates, and identifiers used to distinguish notes. Your own note content may include personal information; you decide what to put in it.

Local storage

Notes are stored in the app’s local database on your Android device. Completed or resolved notes remain in History until you delete them. The current app disables automatic Android app backup and does not provide cloud synchronization.

Export and Import

Export creates a plain JSON file containing your notes, including History, comments, categories, dates, status, and note identifiers. Android’s file picker lets you choose where to save it. Export files are not encrypted by the app.

Import reads a JSON file you select through Android’s file picker. The app validates the file and asks for confirmation before adding notes to the local database. Notes with identifiers already present are skipped.

A file location offered by Android may belong to a cloud storage provider. If you select that location, that provider may store or transfer the file under its own terms and privacy policy. The app itself does not automatically upload your notes. Treat exported files as private and share them only deliberately.

Data sharing

The developer does not receive, sell, or share the contents of your notes. You may choose to share an exported file outside the app. Public support issues also share whatever you post, as explained below.

Third-party services, analytics, and advertising

The current app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or remote crash-reporting service. Its third-party libraries provide local interface, database, and animation functionality; the app’s animations are bundled locally. No third-party data-collection SDK or network service is integrated into the current app.

Android, Google Play, and any document provider you choose are separate services governed by their own policies. This policy describes what 1000 Miles itself does.

Website and public support

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics, advertising, cookies, external fonts, or embedded tracking tools. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate its services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support is available through the public website repository’s GitHub Issues. Posting an issue requires a GitHub account, and your issue, username, and any attachments may be public. Do not post note contents, exported backups, credentials, or other personal information. The Android app itself does not require a GitHub account.

Children’s privacy

The app does not implement age-based profiles or collect information from children through an account or online service. Its on-device storage works in the same way for all users. Avoid including children’s personal information in public support issues or shared export files.

Data retention and deletion

Local notes remain until you delete them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Removing the app or deleting a note does not delete previously exported files. You must remove those copies from the locations where you saved or shared them; cloud providers may have their own retention rules.

Security

Notes are stored within the app’s device-local storage. The app does not add its own database encryption, and exported JSON files are readable by anyone who can access them. Use your device’s screen lock and security updates, and keep backups in a location you trust. No storage or transfer method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If app data handling changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Review this policy when updating the app.

Contact and support

For questions about this policy or the app, visit the Support page. Please keep public messages free of personal information.
