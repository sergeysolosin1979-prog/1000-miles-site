A little help
along the way.

Questions, feedback, or a step that doesn’t work as expected? You can reach the developer through our public support tracker.

Ask a question or report a problem

Use the Issues section of the public 1000 Miles website repository. Check existing issues first, then describe what happened, what you expected, your app version, and your Android version.

Open public support tracker ↗

Keep private information private. GitHub issues are public. Do not attach exported notes, backups, passwords, or personal information. Remove private details from any screenshot. A GitHub account is needed to post an issue.

Keeping a copy of your notes

In the app, open the gear menu and choose Export to save a JSON copy. Choose Import to restore notes from a compatible file; confirm before they are added. Keep exported files somewhere you trust.

Release availability

1000 Miles is currently preparing for Google Play closed testing. There is no public download link on this website yet.

Privacy questions

Read the Privacy Policy for local storage, export and import, retention, and public support details. You can ask a general privacy question through the support tracker without including personal data.
